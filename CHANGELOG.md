# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The **PRO** controller layout is now called **Controller Support Plus**. If you were using it, you still are.
- **Auto-pickup** is now an option in Settings (the new **Misc** tab), and it stays the way you set it between play sessions. On the Controller Support Plus layout, blocking while you sprint no longer turns it on or off by accident, and the controller picture no longer lists it on the left stick.
- **Controller cursor** can now be turned off (General tab).
- **D-pad shortcuts** (Hotbar tab, was "LT + D-pad function") picks what the D-pad does on its own: Item shortcuts by default, or Function shortcuts.
- **LT + D-pad shortcuts** (Hotbar tab) is new: untick it and holding LT no longer changes what the D-pad does. Next to it you can see which set LT gives you.
- **LT + Select** to open Settings moved to the General tab. The last tab is now called **Misc**.
- Finishing **Rerun setup** now takes you straight back into the game instead of back to Settings.
- Fixed: with some mods that use Jotunn, LT shortcuts also did the game's normal action (LT + D-pad down made you sit, LT + A made you jump).

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- **Controller Hotbar**: eight items on your HUD, four on the D-pad and four on LT + the face buttons, in three layouts: Cross, Compact cross and Row.
- **LT shortcuts**: hold LT and use the D-pad for consumables, emotes, sitting and the hammer, or switch them to item shortcuts.
- **Inventory and menus**: a Controller Cursor in the bag, chests, the build menu and pickers, a button guide under the bag, quick moves between bag and chest, and a fast stack split.
- **Object slots**: mannequins, item stands, item frames and boss altars open like a chest.
- **HUD presets**: Vanilla, Bottom HUD and Action RPG.
- **Optional styles**: Detailed or Slim buffs and debuffs, a modern pickup feed, and a radial equip ring around the crosshair. Each can stay vanilla.
- **PRO controller layout**: dodge on B, sneak on hold B, Forsaken power on LB + RB, sheathe on right stick click.
- **First-launch setup** to pick your hotbar and HUD layout with live previews.
- **In-game settings window** from the pause menu, with four tabs.
